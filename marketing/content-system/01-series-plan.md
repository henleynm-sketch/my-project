# Series Plan — Season 1

**Goal:** someone in Durham, the Kawarthas or Muskoka planning a build sees 5 reels
and thinks *"these guys have a real process. Call them."*

**Voice:** Nick talking like he talks. Short sentences. No sales language.
Show the work, explain the why.

## The 4 series

| Code | Series | What it is | Length | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **What We Build** | One reel per job type | 45–60s | Weeks 1–4 |
| **B** | **How We Build** | Our SOP, stage by stage | 45–75s | Weekly |
| **C** | **On Site** | Follow one real custom home start to finish | 30–60s | Every milestone |
| **D** | **Builder Tips** | One quick lesson, one hook | 15–30s | Fillers / weekly |

Every reel ends pointing to the next one: *"Next: how we price a custom home."*
That's what makes it a series and not random posts.

## C — On Site: one custom home, start to finish

**Pick one active custom build with a client who's happy to be filmed** (release signed).
Film at every milestone. These episodes also feed B8.

| # | Milestone | SOP | Must-get shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Meet the lot + the plan | 1.2 / 2.1 | Nick on the empty lot holding the plans |
| C2 | Clearing & digging | 3.7, 3.10 | Excavator first bucket |
| C3 | Footings & foundation | 3.11–3.12 | Concrete pour, forms stripped |
| C4 | Framing goes up | 3.13–3.14 | Timelapse from one fixed spot, every visit |
| C5 | Dried in: windows + roof | 3.15–3.16 | Window delivery, shingles going on |
| C6 | Behind the walls | 3.18–3.20, 4.1 | Rough-ins labelled, insulation |
| C7 | Finishes | 4.2–4.9 | Cabinets in, tile, first light switch on |
| C8 | Reveal + keys | 5.4 | Client walkthrough reaction (with release) |

**Fixed-spot rule:** pick 2 spots on site day 1 (front corner + one interior).
Film 10s from the exact same spot every visit. That's your before/after and timelapse.

## D — Builder Tips (fast fillers)

One idea. 15–30s. Great for weeks when site footage is thin.

1. "Order these 4 things first" — windows, doors, cabinets, appliances (long-lead)
2. "What's an allowance?" — and why it's not a guess
3. "What a change order actually is"
4. "Septic in the Kawarthas — budget for it early"
5. "3 questions to ask any builder before you sign"
6. "Why your builder should ask about your budget on call #1"
7. "What a design fee actually buys you"
8. "The inspection nobody tells you about" — pick a real one per job
