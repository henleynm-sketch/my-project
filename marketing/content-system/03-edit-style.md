# Edit Style — how every reel looks

**Rule #1: they decide in 1.5 seconds.** The hook carries the whole reel.

## Structure (45–60s reel)

| Time | Beat | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | **Hook** | White box text, top third. Nick already talking. No logo intro. |
| 3–10s | **Why it matters** | Talking head + first B-roll |
| 10–45s | **The meat** | Cut to B-roll every 3–5s. Key term pops as text. |
| 45–55s | **Payoff** | One-line takeaway |
| last 3s | **End card** | CTA + handle. "Next: …" |

## Visual rules

- **9:16, 1080×1920.** Horizontal clips get cropped (or blurred fill for plans/screens).
- **Captions always on.** 85% watch muted. 3 words at a time, UPPERCASE, current word in accent yellow.
- **Safe zones:** nothing important in the **top 250px** or **bottom 560px** (Instagram covers it).
- **Cut every 2–5 seconds.** Talking head can run longer if it's good.
- **No dead air.** Pauses > 0.45s and "umm/uh" are cut automatically.
- **Music low** (ducked under voice). Instrumental only. Use Instagram's licensed audio for trending tracks — add it in the app at post time.
- **No transitions, no swooshes, no star wipes.** Hard cuts.

## Text overlays

- **Hook:** ≤ 8 words. Question or a strong claim.
- **Mid-reel pops:** a number or a term ("4 months", "12 selections", "fixed price").
- Keep text off faces.

## Hook formulas that work for builders

1. **Myth:** "Most people think ___. It's actually ___."
2. **Number:** "___ things we do before we pour a foundation"
3. **Behind the scenes:** "What your builder does before you see anything"
4. **Mistake:** "The #1 mistake people make building a custom home"
5. **Time:** "A full custom home in 60 seconds"
6. **Direct:** "If you're building in the Kawarthas, watch this"

## Post caption

- Line 1 = restate the hook (it shows in the feed preview)
- 2–4 short lines of value
- CTA: DM "BUILD" / link in bio
- Hashtags: 5–8, local + niche (in `brand.json`)
