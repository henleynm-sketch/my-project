# Shot Lists — what to film

## Phone settings (set once)

- **Vertical.** Always. Hold the phone upright.
- **4K, 30fps.** iPhone: Settings → Camera → Record Video → 4K at 30.
- **Lock exposure:** tap-and-hold on the subject before you hit record.
- **Clean the lens.** Job sites = dusty lens = blurry reel.
- **Mic:** a clip-on wireless mic (DJI Mic / Rode Wireless Go) is the single best upgrade.
  Wind on an open lot kills audio. No mic → stand out of the wind, phone close.

## Every shoot: the 4 shot types

| Type | What | How long | How many |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A-roll** | Nick talking to camera | Whole topic, keep rolling | 1–3 takes |
| **B-roll** | The work: hands, tools, materials, trades | 5–10s each | **10+** |
| **Wide** | Establishing shot of the site / room | 10s, slow walk or still | 2 |
| **Fixed-spot** | Same spot, same framing as last visit (Series C) | 10s still | 2 |

**B-roll is what makes it look professional.** You can never have too much.

## Talking (A-roll) rules

1. **Say the hook first.** The first sentence is the on-screen hook.
2. **One take, keep going.** Messed up a line? Pause, say it again. The editor cuts the pause and the bad take.
3. **Phone at eye level, arm's length or on a tripod.** Not looking up your nose.
4. **Light in front of you.** Never stand with the sun or a window behind you.
5. **End with the next episode:** *"Next one — how we price a build."*

## B-roll checklist (grab what's happening that day)

- [ ] Hands doing the work (nailing, measuring, cutting, laying tile)
- [ ] Machines (excavator bucket, pump truck, lift, crane)
- [ ] Materials arriving (lumber drop, window delivery, cabinet boxes)
- [ ] Details (a clean joint, a level bubble, a fresh pour surface)
- [ ] People walking the site (wearing PPE)
- [ ] Plans / screens (floorplan on iPad, selections board, permit stamp)
- [ ] Slow pan of a finished room
- [ ] Drone wide (if available) — top-down of the lot

## Per-episode shot lists

### A-series (job types)
| Ep | Location | A-roll | Must-have B-roll |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 Jobs we take on | Office or truck | Hook + list the 5 types | 1 clip from each job type (pull from archive) |
| A2 Custom homes | Active custom build | Design-build, timeline, rural lots | Lot wide, framing, finished home pan |
| A3 Additions | Addition site at the tie-in | Why tie-ins are hard | Old wall opened up, new framing meeting old |
| A4 Major renos | Gutted room | Unknowns behind walls | Demo, old wiring/ducts exposed, finished space |
| A5 Garden suites | Backyard suite site | Zoning, servicing, uses | Footprint, service trench, finished exterior |
| A6 Commercial | Commercial site | Working around a business | Trades at work, signage (if client OK), finished space |

### B-series (process)
| Ep | Location | Must-have B-roll |
|---|---|---|
| B1 First call | Truck → client property | Phone ringing/call, Nick walking a lot, measuring tape, photos on phone |
| B2 Design proposal | Office / client's kitchen table | Proposal document (blur numbers), handshake |
| B3 Floorplan | Office | Screen recording of plans, markups, before/after layout |
| B4 Selections | Supplier showroom / selections meeting | Window samples, tile boards, cabinet doors, appliance showroom |
| B5 Permits | Office + site | Drawings with engineer stamp (blur names), septic test pit, permit card on site |
| B6 Pricing | Office | Estimate workbook **scrolling fast, numbers blurred**, cost code list |
| B7 Pre-con | Site + office | Delivery schedule, PO screen (blurred), site fencing going up, kickoff meeting |
| B8 Build in 60s | — | Built from Series C fixed-spot footage |
| B9 Handoff | Finished home | Final walk, keys handover, client reaction (release signed) |

### C-series (On Site)
Every visit: **A-roll update (30s) + fixed-spot shots + 10 B-roll clips of whatever's happening.**

### D-series (tips)
A-roll only is fine. One B-roll clip of the thing you're talking about.

## Before you leave site

- [ ] Hook said on camera
- [ ] 10+ B-roll clips
- [ ] Fixed-spot shots (Series C)
- [ ] No addresses, plates, faces without release, or unsafe work in frame
- [ ] Dump clips into the episode's `raw/` folder that day
