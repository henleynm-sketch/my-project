# Henley Contracting — Brand Messaging Guide

## Mission Statement

We build the spaces where families grow together.

At Henley Contracting, we're a family business that builds for families. Every home we create is designed around how people actually live — how they gather, celebrate, raise kids, and make memories. We don't just build structures. We build the next chapter.

---

## Core Taglines

**Primary:**
> Build. Grow. Together.

**Alternates:**
> Where families come home.
> Built for the life you're growing into.
> Your family is growing. Your home should too.
> We don't just build homes. We build what's next.

## Brand Pillars

### 1. Family First
We're a family business building for families. We understand what it means to outgrow a space, to need room for the next kid, the next chapter, the next phase. We've lived it ourselves.

**Content themes:** Behind-the-scenes of the team, multi-generational builds, growing families moving in, "why we do this" stories

### 2. The Process Matters
We believe how you build is just as important as what you build. Our process is transparent, organized, and built on trust. Clients aren't left guessing — they're part of the build from day one.

**Content themes:** Site walkthroughs, BuilderTrend updates, timeline breakdowns, "how we build" series, trade partner spotlights

### 3. Built for Growth
Families grow. Businesses grow. Communities grow. We design and build spaces that grow with you — not homes you'll outgrow in five years.

**Content themes:** Before/after reveals, flex spaces, future-proofed designs, additions and renovations that transformed how families live

### 4. Ontario Roots
We know Ontario. We build to the Ontario Building Code. We work with local trades, local suppliers, and we understand the climate, the soil, the regulations. This is home for us too.

**Content themes:** Local community involvement, Ontario-specific building challenges, seasonal construction content, local trade partner features

## Brand Voice

### Tone
- **Confident, not cocky** — We know what we're doing. We don't need to brag.
- **Warm, not soft** — We're approachable but we're builders. There's grit here.
- **Direct, not corporate** — Talk like a person. No jargon. No fluff.
- **Passionate, not salesy** — We love what we do. That should come through naturally.

### Do Say
- "We build spaces where families grow together"
- "Here's how we do it and why"
- "This is what gets us excited"
- "Your family deserves a home that grows with you"
- "We're builders. This is what we do."

### Don't Say
- "Luxury custom homes" (everyone says this — it means nothing)
- "World-class craftsmanship" (show it, don't say it)
- "We're the best" (let the work speak)
- "Clients" when you can say "families"
- Corporate jargon — no "synergies," "leveraging," "turnkey solutions"

## Audience Profiles

### Primary: Growing Families (Age 30-45)
- Outgrowing their current home
- First custom build or major renovation
- Nervous about the process, excited about the result
- Want to understand what they're paying for
- Value transparency, communication, and trust

### Secondary: Established Families (Age 45-60)
- Empty nesters reimagining their space
- Building their forever home
- Renovation or addition to an existing home
- More experienced, higher expectations
- Value quality, reputation, and process

### Tertiary: Small Business Owners
- Need commercial space that reflects their brand
- Growing business, growing team
- Understand the "growth" message personally
- Value efficiency, timelines, and professionalism
