# Nick Henley — LinkedIn Personal Brand Strategy

## Positioning

**Nick Henley** — Builder. Business owner. Family man.
Sharing the real story of running a construction company in Ontario — the wins, the lessons, the process, and why it all comes back to family.

---

## Content Pillars (Rotate Weekly)

### 1. The Builder's Perspective (Monday/Tuesday)
Why you do what you do. Lessons from the field. What most people don't understand about construction.

**Examples:**
- "Most people think building a home starts with a blueprint. It actually starts with a conversation about how your family lives."
- "I've been in construction for X years. Here's the one thing I wish every homeowner knew before they started a build."
- "We walked a job site today and I noticed something that reminded me why process matters..."

### 2. Behind the Business (Wednesday/Thursday)
Running a family construction business in Ontario. The real stuff — hiring, managing trades, growing a team, dealing with challenges.

**Examples:**
- "We lost a bid last week. Here's what I learned from it."
- "Hiring in construction right now is [hard/changing/exciting]. Here's what we're doing differently."
- "Three years ago we were a crew of 4. Now we're [X]. Here's what changed."

### 3. Family + Purpose (Friday/Weekend)
The personal side. Why family drives everything. The connection between building homes and building a life.

**Examples:**
- "I build homes for a living. But the reason I build is standing right beside me in this photo."
- "My dad taught me that a house isn't just walls and a roof. It's where your kids take their first steps."
- "We're a family business. That's not a marketing line — my [brother/wife/dad] is on site with me every day."

## Post Formats That Work on LinkedIn

### 1. Story Post (Highest engagement)
```
Short hook line that stops the scroll.

Then 2-3 short paragraphs telling a real story.
Something specific. A moment on site. A conversation
with a client. A lesson learned the hard way.

End with a takeaway or question.

What's a lesson your work has taught you about life?
```

### 2. Photo + Insight
```
[Photo from a job site, a finished space, or the team]

One line about what you're looking at.

Then the deeper insight — what this moment means,
what it taught you, or why it matters.
```

### 3. Carousel / Document Post
```
Slide 1: Bold statement or question
Slide 2-6: The breakdown (process, tips, lessons)
Slide 7: Summary + CTA
```

**Carousel ideas:**
- "5 things every family should know before building a custom home"
- "Our build process from first call to move-in day"
- "What actually happens during a site walkthrough"
- "The real cost of building in Ontario (what nobody talks about)"

### 4. Video (Highest reach)
```
- 60-90 seconds max
- Talk directly to camera
- On a job site or in your truck
- One idea per video
- Hook in first 3 seconds
```

## Posting Cadence

| Day | Content Type | Pillar |
|-----|-------------|--------|
| Monday | Story post or photo + insight | Builder's Perspective |
| Wednesday | Carousel or video | Behind the Business |
| Friday | Personal story or reflection | Family + Purpose |

**Minimum:** 3 posts/week
**Ideal:** 5 posts/week (add Tuesday + Thursday with lighter content — a photo, a quick thought, a reshare with commentary)

---

## Engagement Strategy

### Daily (10 min)
- Reply to every comment on your posts
- Comment on 5 posts from people in your network (other builders, realtors, architects, local business owners)
- Comments should add value, not just "Great post!"

### Weekly
- Connect with 10-15 new people (local realtors, architects, designers, potential clients in Ontario)
- Share or comment on 1 industry article with your take

### Monthly
- One longer "reflection" post about the business journey
- Feature a team member, trade partner, or completed project
