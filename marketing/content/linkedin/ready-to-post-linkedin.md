## Post 4 — Bonus (Process / Educational)

**Hook:** Building a custom home in Ontario? Here's what nobody tells you.

I've been building custom homes in Ontario for years. Here are 5 things I wish every family knew before they started:

1️⃣ **Your lot matters more than you think.** Soil conditions, grading, setbacks, and services can add $30-100K+ to your budget before a single wall goes up. Get a site assessment early.

2️⃣ **The Ontario Building Code isn't optional.** And it shouldn't be. It's there to protect your family. A good builder doesn't cut corners on code — they know it inside out.

3️⃣ **"Allowances" can be a trap.** If your builder gives you a $5,000 allowance for tile and your taste runs at $15/sq ft, you're going to blow that budget. Nail down your selections early.

4️⃣ **HST is 13%.** On everything. A $500,000 build has $65,000 in HST. Make sure your budget accounts for it from day one. (There are rebate programs — ask your builder and accountant about them.)

5️⃣ **The cheapest bid is rarely the best bid.** I've seen families choose the lowest quote only to spend more fixing problems later. Compare scope, not just price.

Building a home should be exciting, not stressful. The more you know going in, the better the experience.
