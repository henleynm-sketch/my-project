# Henley Contracting — Social Media Strategy
## Instagram | Facebook | TikTok

---

## Platform Breakdown

### Instagram (@henleycontracting)
**Purpose:** Visual portfolio + process storytelling
**Best for:** Project photos, Reels, before/after, carousels
**Audience:** Homeowners, design enthusiasts, realtors, local community
**Posting:** 4-5x/week (mix of feed posts, Stories, Reels)

### Facebook (Henley Contracting)
**Purpose:** Community trust + longer-form content
**Best for:** Project updates, client testimonials, community involvement, events
**Audience:** Homeowners 35-60, local community, referral network
**Posting:** 3-4x/week (can cross-post from Instagram + add FB-specific content)

### TikTok (@henleycontracting)
**Purpose:** Reach + education + personality
**Best for:** Quick tips, satisfying construction clips, day-in-the-life, trending audio
**Audience:** Younger homeowners 25-40, future clients, aspiring builders
**Posting:** 3-5x/week (short video only)

## Content Pillars

### 1. The Build (40% of content)
The work itself. Show what you do.

**Post types:**
- Progress photos and videos
- Before/after reveals (highest engagement)
- Time-lapse clips
- Drone footage of sites
- Detail shots (trim, tile, framing, concrete)
- "Day [X] of this build" series

**Example captions:**
- "Day 42. The framing is up and this one is starting to feel like a home. 🏗️ #HenleyBuilds"
- "Before → After. This family needed more room to grow. We gave them 1,200 sq ft of it."
- "There's something about seeing a foundation go in. This is where it all starts."

### 2. The Process (25% of content)
How you build. Why it matters. Educate and build trust.

**Post types:**
- "How we do [X]" explainers
- Site walkthrough videos
- Material selection process
- Meeting with clients (with permission)
- BuilderTrend screenshots showing transparency
- Trade partner spotlights

**Example captions:**
- "People ask us what makes Henley different. It starts here — our clients see everything. Every update, every cost, every decision. That's how we build trust. #HenleyProcess"
- "This is what a pre-drywall walkthrough looks like. We walk every family through their home before the walls go up so they can see exactly what's behind them."
- "Meet [Trade Partner Name]. They've been doing our [electrical/plumbing/tile] for [X] years. Great builds start with great people."

### 3. The Why (20% of content)
Why you do this. The families. The purpose. The heart.

**Post types:**
- Move-in day moments
- Client stories and testimonials
- Family moments in finished spaces
- "Why we build" reflections
- Team and company culture
- Community involvement

**Example captions:**
- "This is why we do it. Move-in day for the [Family Name]. Three kids, a dog, and a home that finally fits their life. Welcome home. 🏡"
- "We're not just building houses. We're building the space where your kids take their first steps, where you host Thanksgiving, where you finally have room to breathe."
- "Every project starts with a family telling us about the life they want to build. That conversation is our favourite part."

### 4. Personality + Fun (15% of content)
Show the human side. Be real. Have fun.

**Post types:**
- Job site bloopers
- Team lunch / coffee runs
- Weather challenges (Ontario winters)
- Satisfying construction clips (with trending audio)
- "Things builders understand" relatable content
- Day-in-the-life vlogs

**Example captions:**
- "Ontario in February. Still building. ❄️🔨"
- "POV: You're a builder and it just started raining during a pour 🫠"
- "Friday energy on the Henley crew. Have a great weekend everyone."

## Content Production Tips

### Photos
- Natural light whenever possible
- Wide shots to show scale + detail shots to show quality
- Include people when you can (team, clients with permission)
- Clean job sites photograph better — take 2 minutes to tidy before shooting
- Golden hour shots of exteriors = money content

### Video
- Horizontal for YouTube/Facebook, vertical for Reels/TikTok/Stories
- First 3 seconds must hook — start with action or a bold statement
- Add captions to all video (80% of people watch without sound)
- Keep Reels and TikToks under 90 seconds
- Drone footage is premium — use it for reveals and project overviews

### Consistency
- Use consistent colour treatment on photos (pick a filter/preset and stick with it)
- Logo watermark on finished project photos (subtle, corner placement)
- Same profile photo across all platforms
- Bio should include: What you do + Where + How to contact
