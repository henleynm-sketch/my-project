Because building a home should feel exciting, not stressful. And that only happens when you trust the process and the people behind it.

This is how we build at Henley."

## SERIES 2: "How We Build" (Educational — All Platforms)
**Format:** Nick on site explaining a specific process, 60-90 seconds
**Purpose:** Show expertise. Build trust. Give value.

### Topics:

**1. "What actually happens before we break ground"**
- Site assessment, soil testing, permits, design finalization
- "Most of the work happens before you see any action on site"

**2. "Why the foundation matters more than you think"**
- Ontario frost depth, waterproofing, weeping tile
- Show an actual foundation being poured or inspected

**3. "What's behind your walls"**
- Insulation, vapour barrier, electrical, plumbing
- "You'll never see this again, but it's what makes your home work"

**4. "How we keep your project on budget"**
- BuilderTrend walkthrough
- Change order process
- "No surprises. That's the rule."

**5. "The walkthrough your builder should be doing"**
- Pre-drywall walkthrough with the homeowner
- "This is your chance to see everything before it's covered up"

**6. "How we choose our trades"**
- Vetting process, relationships, accountability
- "A build is only as good as the people doing the work"

## SERIES 4: "Project Showcase" (YouTube + LinkedIn + Instagram)
**Format:** 3-5 minute mini-documentary per project
**Purpose:** Portfolio content. Show the full story from start to finish.

### Structure for Each Episode:

```
1. THE FAMILY (30 sec)
   - Who they are, why they came to Henley
   - What wasn't working about their current situation

2. THE VISION (30 sec)
   - What they wanted
   - Design decisions and trade-offs

3. THE BUILD (90-120 sec)
   - Time-lapse + key moments
   - Challenges and how you solved them
   - Drone footage, detail shots

4. THE REVEAL (30-60 sec)
   - Finished space
   - Family experiencing it for the first time
   - Their reaction

5. THE REFLECTION (30 sec)
   - Client testimonial
   - Nick's closing thought
```

## Quick Video Tips for Nick

### Before you shoot:
- Wipe the camera lens (seriously — job site dust)
- Face natural light or a window
- Frame from chest up for talking head
- Stabilize your phone — lean it against something or use a tripod

### While shooting:
- Look at the camera lens, not the screen
- Talk like you're explaining to a friend, not presenting
- One idea per video — don't cram
- Pause for 2 seconds at the start and end (easier to edit)
- It's okay to mess up — authenticity > perfection

### After shooting:
- Add captions (CapCut or Instagram's auto-captions)
- Keep the first 3 seconds punchy — hook or action
- Post natively to each platform (don't share TikTok links to Instagram)
- Best posting times for Ontario: 7-8am, 12-1pm, 7-9pm
