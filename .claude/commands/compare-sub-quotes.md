Compare sub-trade quotes for a specific trade on a project.

Steps:
1. Ask which project and which trade (e.g., electrical, plumbing, HVAC, concrete)
2. Search email via MS 365 for quotes from subs for that trade and project
3. Extract pricing from each quote — look for total price, scope inclusions, exclusions, and qualifications
4. Build a comparison table: Sub Name | Total Price | Scope Notes | Inclusions | Exclusions | Expiry Date
5. Highlight the lowest and highest bids
6. Flag any subs whose scope doesn't match or who have significant exclusions
7. Recommend which quote to carry in the estimate and why
