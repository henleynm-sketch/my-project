# Follow-Up Message Templates — Henley Contracting

Use these AFTER someone accepts your connection request. Wait 1-2 days after they accept before sending.

---

## 1. Thank You + Soft Intro (Universal)

```
Thanks for connecting, [First Name]! I run Henley Contracting — we specialize in custom home builds, additions, and full renovations across Oshawa, Whitby, and Kawartha Lakes.

If you ever come across anyone looking to build or renovate, I'd be happy to chat with them. And if there's ever anything I can do for you, don't hesitate to reach out!
```

## 2. Realtor — Referral Partnership

```
Thanks for connecting, [First Name]! I know a great renovation or pre-sale refresh can make a huge difference for your listings. We've helped homeowners in [area] add serious value before selling.

Would you be open to a quick coffee or call sometime? I'd love to learn more about your business and see if there's a way we can send clients each other's way.
```

## 3. Mortgage Broker — Referral Partnership

```
Appreciate the connection, [First Name]! Many of our clients are first-time builders or doing major renos and need solid mortgage advice. I'd love to have someone reliable to recommend.

Would you be open to a quick chat about how we might refer clients to each other?
```

## 4. Fellow Trades — Subcontractor Relationship

```
Thanks for connecting, [First Name]! We're always looking for reliable [trade] pros for our projects in the [Oshawa/Whitby/Kawartha Lakes] area.

If you're ever looking for extra work or want to collaborate on projects, let's chat. What's your availability looking like these days?
```

## 5. Past Client — Review & Referral Ask

```
Hey [First Name], hope you're enjoying [the new kitchen / the renovation / the new home]! It was a great project to work on.

If you have a minute, a quick recommendation here on LinkedIn would mean the world to us. And if you know anyone thinking about building or renovating, we'd love an introduction!
```

## 6. Re-Engagement (Haven't Spoken in a While)

```
Hey [First Name], hope things are going well! Just wanted to pop in and say hi. We've been busy with some exciting projects in [area] lately.

If you ever need anything building or renovation related, or know someone who does, don't hesitate to reach out!
```
