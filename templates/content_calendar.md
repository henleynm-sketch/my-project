# LinkedIn Content Calendar — Henley Contracting

Post 3-4 times per week to stay visible. Mix these content types to keep your feed interesting.

---

## Weekly Posting Schedule

| Day       | Content Type              | Example                                                  |
|-----------|---------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| Monday    | Project Showcase          | Before/after photos with a short story about the project |
| Wednesday | Educational / Tips        | "3 things to know before renovating your kitchen"        |
| Thursday  | Behind the Scenes         | Jobsite progress, team at work, material selections      |
| Saturday  | Community / Personal      | Local event, charity work, weekend thoughts              |

---

## Content Ideas by Category

### Project Showcases (Before & After)
- Kitchen renovations
- Bathroom remodels
- Basement finishes
- Home additions
- Custom new builds (progress series)
- Exterior / curb appeal transformations
- Deck and outdoor living spaces

**Template:**
```
[Before/After photos]

This [project type] in [Oshawa/Whitby/Kawartha Lakes] was a [fun/challenging/rewarding] one.

The homeowner wanted [goal]. We [what you did].

The result? [Outcome — increased home value, dream kitchen, etc.]

Thinking about a renovation? Let's talk.

#HenleyContracting #HomeRenovation #[Oshawa/Whitby/KawarthaLakes] #DurhamRegion #CustomHomes
```

### Educational Posts
- "5 signs your home needs a foundation repair"
- "What to expect during a full home renovation timeline"
- "How to choose the right contractor (yes, even if it's not us)"
- "Permit or no permit? When you need one in Ontario"
- "The real cost of a kitchen renovation in 2026"
- "New build vs. renovation: which is right for you?"
- "Energy-efficient upgrades that actually pay for themselves"

**Template:**
```
[Topic] — here's what most homeowners don't know:

1. [Point]
2. [Point]
3. [Point]

We've seen this [X] times on jobsites across Durham Region and Kawartha Lakes.

Got questions? Drop them in the comments or send me a message.

#HenleyContracting #HomeBuildingTips #[LocalArea] #OntarioContractor
```

### Behind the Scenes
- Framing going up on a new build
- Team photos on the jobsite
- Material deliveries and selections
- Problem-solving moments (how you fixed an unexpected issue)
- Tools and techniques

**Template:**
```
[Photo/video from the jobsite]

Here's a look at [what's happening] on our latest project in [area].

[1-2 sentences about what's interesting or notable]

Love what we do. More updates coming soon.

#HenleyContracting #BuildingHomes #BehindTheScenes #[LocalArea]
```

### Community & Personal
- Sponsoring a local sports team or event
- Volunteering or charity work
- Local business shout-outs
- Milestones (years in business, number of homes built)
- Lessons learned as a business owner

### Engagement Posts
- "What's the one thing you'd change about your home?"
- "Homeowners: what's your biggest renovation fear?"
- Poll: "Kitchen reno — white cabinets or wood tone?"
- "Tag a realtor who goes above and beyond for their clients"
