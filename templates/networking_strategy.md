# LinkedIn Networking Strategy — Henley Contracting

## Goal
Make Henley Contracting the go-to name for home building and renovations in **Oshawa, Whitby, and Kawartha Lakes** through strategic LinkedIn networking.

---

## Target Audiences (Priority Order)

### Tier 1 — Referral Partners (highest ROI)
These people send you clients repeatedly:
- **Realtors** — they advise buyers/sellers on renovations constantly
- **Mortgage brokers** — clients getting financing for builds/renos
- **Insurance adjusters** — fire/water damage restoration referrals

### Tier 2 — Collaborators
These people work alongside you on projects:
- **Interior designers & architects** — they need builders to execute
- **Trades & subcontractors** — electricians, plumbers, HVAC, roofers
- **Landscapers** — often paired with exterior renos

### Tier 3 — Community Presence
These connections raise your local visibility:
- **Local business owners** — cross-promotion opportunities
- **Municipal staff** — building inspectors, planning dept contacts
- **Community organizations** — BIA, chamber of commerce members
- **Local media / bloggers** — coverage opportunities

### Tier 4 — Direct Clients
- **Homeowners in target areas** — harder to find on LinkedIn but worth connecting when you spot them

---

## Weekly Action Plan

### Daily (10-15 minutes)
- [ ] Send 5-10 connection requests with personalized messages
- [ ] Respond to all messages and comments
- [ ] Like/comment on 3-5 posts from your network

### Weekly
- [ ] Post 3-4 times (see content calendar)
- [ ] Check follow-up tracker and send due messages
- [ ] Send 2-3 follow-up messages to new connections
- [ ] Share or comment on a local community post

### Monthly
- [ ] Review contact tracker stats
- [ ] Set up 1-2 coffee meetings with referral partners
- [ ] Ask 1-2 satisfied clients for LinkedIn recommendations
- [ ] Review what posts performed best and do more of those

---

## LinkedIn Profile Optimization Checklist

Make sure your profile sells Henley Contracting before you start outreach:

- [ ] **Headline:** "Custom Home Builder & Renovation Expert | Serving Oshawa, Whitby & Kawartha Lakes | Henley Contracting"
- [ ] **Banner image:** Professional photo of your best completed project
- [ ] **Profile photo:** Professional headshot (or you on a jobsite looking sharp)
- [ ] **About section:** Tell your story — why you started, what you specialize in, areas you serve, and a call to action
- [ ] **Featured section:** Pin your best before/after post, a client testimonial, or your website
- [ ] **Experience:** List Henley Contracting with a strong description and project highlights
- [ ] **Skills:** Add relevant skills (Home Renovation, Construction Management, Project Management, etc.)
- [ ] **Recommendations:** Get 3-5 from past clients and trade partners
- [ ] **Location:** Set to your primary service area
- [ ] **Contact info:** Website, phone, email all visible

## Measuring Success

Track these monthly:
- **Connection acceptance rate** — aim for 40%+
- **Conversations started** — aim for 5-10/month
- **Referral partners established** — aim for 2-3 new ones/month
- **Inbound messages** — should grow over time
- **Post engagement** — likes, comments, shares trending up
- **Leads generated** — the bottom line

---

## Key Principles

1. **Be genuine, not salesy.** Nobody likes cold pitches. Build real relationships.
2. **Give before you ask.** Share their posts, refer them business, be helpful first.
3. **Consistency beats intensity.** 15 minutes daily beats 2 hours once a month.
4. **Local is your superpower.** Big companies can't compete on local relationships.
5. **Show your work.** Before/after photos and jobsite content build trust faster than any ad.
6. **Follow up.** Most people give up after one message. The second and third touchpoints are where relationships form.
